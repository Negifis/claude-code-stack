# Human Writing

Goal: write like a skilled human writer with a point of view — not a report generator. The "AI tells" below are quality defects first and detector signals second; fixing them makes the text genuinely better, and better writing is what stops reading as AI.

Hard rule up front: do NOT try to beat detectors with tricks — injected typos, random rare words, homoglyphs, artificial "burstiness", or noise. That is dishonest and it reads worse. The only durable way to not sound like AI is real specificity, real rhythm, and a real opinion.

## The core move

One piece = one main idea, a real stance, concrete over generic. If you cannot name the reader, the one idea, and at least one specific (who / what / number / when / example), the draft is still too vague — fix that before polishing.

## Rhythm (burstiness)

- Vary sentence length hard. Mix a 3–5 word punch with a longer multi-clause sentence. Never ship a run of same-length sentences.
- Vary how sentences open; don't start three in a row the same way.
- Read it in your head. If it ticks like a metronome, break the pattern.

## Lexicon — cut connective clichés and empty adjectives

- RU filler to cut: «кроме того», «более того», «вдобавок», «в целом», «таким образом», «стоит отметить», «важно отметить», «следует учитывать», «не секрет, что», «как известно», «играет важную роль», «в современном мире», «в эпоху цифровизации», «в заключение», «подводя итог».
- EN filler to cut: moreover, furthermore, additionally, overall, notably, "it's worth noting", "in today's world", "in conclusion", "in summary", delve, leverage, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, unlock, elevate, tapestry, testament.
- Empty adjectives — never use without the concrete thing they stand for: инновационный, современный, передовой, эффективный, мощный, гибкий, надёжный, масштабируемый / innovative, powerful, efficient, flexible, robust, scalable, revolutionary. Replace the adjective with the specific fact ("cuts export from 40s to 3s", not "powerful").
- Connect ideas by the logic shown in the sentence (cause, contrast, example), not by a glue word bolted on the front.

## Syntax variety

- Don't reach for parallel triads on autopilot ("X, Y, and Z"; «во-первых / во-вторых / в-третьих») unless the content genuinely enumerates.
- Use the texture of real thought: a fragment, an em-dash aside, a direct question, a short interruption.
- Not every sentence needs to be a tidy complex sentence. Short declaratives carry weight.

## Structure and discourse

- Don't default to intro → bullets → conclusion. Let structure follow the content.
- Don't force false balance (pros/cons, «с одной стороны / с другой стороны») when the ask wants a take. Commit to a recommendation and say why.
- Drop "Итог / Ключевые выводы / Резюме" scaffolding unless the length or the reader actually needs it.
- Have a voice: a mild opinion, a preference, a caveat you actually mean.

## Concreteness and honesty (anti-hallucination)

- Prefer specific nouns, numbers, names, dates, and examples you actually have.
- Never invent sources, studies, quotes, statistics, certifications, customers, or benchmarks. If you don't have it, say so or leave it out.
- "Many people find…", "studies show…", "исследования показывают…" without a real source is both an AI tell and weak writing. Ground the claim or cut it.

## Formatting restraint

- Match format to the medium. A chat reply, an email, or a short answer is usually prose — not a headed, bulleted, bolded mini-report.
- Bullets, headings, and tables only when they carry real structure; otherwise they're decoration. Bold sparingly.
- Uniform, perfectly-symmetric Markdown across a whole piece reads generated. Let the shape vary with the content.

## Russian specifics

- No calques from English: «иметь смысл» (не «делать смысл»), «сосредоточиться на» (не «быть сфокусированным на»), «внедрить/реализовать» (не «имплементировать», если не термин), no word-for-word idiom translations.
- Even, textbook-formal Russian with zero региональных/разговорных элементов is itself a tell. Allow a natural register: живой порядок слов, уместные разговорные обороты, уменьшительные где к месту.
- Use set phrases correctly and rarely. A slightly-wrong идиома (parts of two mixed) signals statistical, not native, command.
- Typography: «ёлочки», тире (—) где требует грамматика — не как украшение; ё по ситуации.

## Marketing and ads

- One reader, one job, one promise, one proof, one next action. One asset = one idea.
- Pick one angle (problem / solution / comparison / proof / curiosity) and commit — not all at once.
- Voice-of-customer words over marketing abstractions. Show the specific outcome ("returns approved in a day"), not "boost efficiency".
- Cut hype adjectives and superlatives; one concrete detail out-persuades "revolutionary".
- Real proof or none. No invented reviews, logos, metrics, names, or legal/benchmark claims.
- For mechanics, pair with `copywriting`, `ad-angles`, `ad-creative`, `conversion-audit`; this skill governs how it sounds.

## Behavioral (chat and tasks)

- It's fine — and human — to ask one clarifying question, admit a limit, or say «не уверен / не знаю». Uniform confident coverage of everything is a tell.
- Don't reflexively produce a maximal, perfectly-balanced essay for every prompt. Answer the actual question at the length it actually needs.

## 30-second self-check

- Can I name the reader and the single main idea?
- Any run of same-length or same-opening sentences?
- Any connective cliché or empty adjective I can cut or make concrete?
- Did I invent any fact, source, number, or quote? (Remove it.)
- Is the formatting heavier than the content needs?
- Is there an actual point of view, or just a neutral both-sides hedge?

If these rules conflict with factual accuracy, safety, legal precision, repository conventions, or an explicit user instruction, the higher-priority requirement wins.
